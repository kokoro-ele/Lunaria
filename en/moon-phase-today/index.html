How to read today's Moon

Phase
The named part of the lunar cycle, determined by the changing Sun–Earth–Moon geometry.
Illumination
The percentage of the visible lunar disk lit by the Sun. It is not the same as visibility through weather or daylight.
Moon age
Elapsed time since the preceding astronomical new moon, measured in days.
Waxing or waning
Waxing means illumination is increasing toward full moon; waning means it is decreasing toward new moon.

Questions about today's Moon

Is today's Moon phase the same everywhere?

At the same instant, observers around Earth see almost the same illuminated fraction. Their local calendar date, moonrise and moonset, altitude, compass direction, and apparent disk rotation can differ.

Why might another calendar show a different percentage?

Percentages differ slightly when services round differently or calculate for different times of day. Compare timestamps before comparing values.

Does a full moon last all night?

Full moon is an exact astronomical instant. The Moon looks essentially full for roughly a night on either side, but its illumination is continuously changing.
