Using this calendar for observation

Near new moon

The night sky is darkest around new moon, although the Moon's separation from the Sun and local twilight still matter.

Near first quarter

Evening viewing is convenient, and shadows near the terminator reveal relief along lunar terrain.

Near full moon

The disk is brightest and rises around sunset, but the high Sun angle can flatten the appearance of craters.

Near last quarter

The Moon is best seen after midnight and into the morning, with a different half of the disk near the terminator.

2026 calendar questions

Why are there 13 full moons in 2026?

A calendar year is longer than twelve average synodic months. In 2026 that spacing places two full-moon instants in May when counted in UTC.

Why does my local calendar show another date?

These entries use UTC. Convert the listed instant to your time zone; dates near 00:00 UTC commonly move to the previous or next local day.

Is the full moon visible from everywhere at that exact time?

No. Full moon describes the phase geometry. At the exact instant it may be below your horizon, blocked by terrain, or obscured by weather.
