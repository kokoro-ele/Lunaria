2027 calendar questions

How many full moons are in 2027?

There are 12 full-moon instants in 2027 when dates are counted in UTC, one in each calendar month.

Why are there two new moons in August?

The synodic month averages about 29.53 days, shorter than August. A new moon occurs early on August 2 and another late on August 31 UTC.

Are these the best times to photograph the Moon?

That depends on the goal. Full moon gives a bright complete disk, while phases nearer the quarters create longer surface shadows and stronger relief.
